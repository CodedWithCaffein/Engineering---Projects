## What this total covers

The subtotal adds the listed item prices before tax, shipping, and order-level adjustments. It excludes the radio transmitter, all 3D-printing filament, and the unrelated $19.99 phone accessory on the ER8 receipt. A design-file price, battery price, and any additional replacement-material charges are not documented here.

Order totals can include shipping, tax, promotions, rewards, or gift-card payments. They should not be added to this item-price subtotal. Gift-card payments are a payment method, rather than a reduction in the value of the parts.

The original ER6 cost $35.56 and the replacement ER8 cost $49.99. Both remain in the spending record; no refund is assumed.

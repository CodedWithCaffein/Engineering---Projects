## Evidence used

The component list and costs were checked against my supplied purchase screenshots, research images, build photos, and the system information collected on October 31, 2026. The [purchase records](receipts.md) establish order dates and totals; the [photos](photos.md) show the build and setup. Research screenshots provide context and are not purchase receipts or current price quotes.

The system check reported a Ryzen 5 9600X, ASUS ROG Strix B650E-I Gaming WiFi motherboard, RTX 3080 with 10 GB graphics memory, approximately 31.66 GB usable system memory, and a C: volume of approximately 930.4 GiB. Usable memory and formatted volume capacity are distinct from the advertised 32 GB kit and 1 TB drive capacities. Raw diagnostics and device identifiers are not included here.
